Return on investment

Monthly ROI

Total investment is Google Ads spend plus the NZ$750/month management retainer. Revenue is booking value tracked from Google Ads. Not affected by the date range above.

MonthAd spendManagementTotal investmentBookingsRevenueNet returnROI

ROI = (revenue − total investment) ÷ total investment. Revenue is GA4 booking value until FareHarbor reconciliation is in place. The retainer started when ads went live on 15 Oct, so October is pro-rated by days live. The current month is month to date, and its retainer is shown in full.

How to read this

Definitions

  • Bookings: the GA4 purchase event from FareHarbor checkout, as attributed to Google Ads. Enquiries and button clicks are not counted.
  • Booking value: the value GA4 sends with each purchase. Not yet checked against FareHarbor, so it may differ for refunds, cancellations, tax or fees.
  • ROAS: booking value divided by ad spend. Cost / booking is blank where there are no bookings.
  • Google credits a booking to the day of the ad click, and keeps attributing for several days, so the most recent days can still rise.
